Built to answer one question

Is the squat rack free? Is the assault bike taped off? What's actually on the floor at this gym?

Search gyms

Find a gym by name, address, chain, or Hong Kong district. Browse without creating an account.

Equipment lists

Open a gym to see machines, racks, cardio, and free weights people have listed on that floor.

Condition & wait

Each review rates condition 1–5 and availability — plenty, some wait, crowded, or out of service.

Contribute

Log in to add a gym, add a piece of equipment, write a review, or upvote a useful report.

Hong Kong sample data

The first release ships with labelled sample gyms in Central, Sha Tin, Mong Kok, and Kwun Tong.

Flutter for iOS & Android

The running app is a Flutter MVP. Catalog data is stored on-device in this first pass.

How it works

The unit of review is a machine at a gym — not a generic star rating for the club.

1. Browse

Open the gym list, search, or filter by district. Seeded listings are marked Sample data.

2. Check the floor

Open a gym for its equipment list and aggregate condition. Open an item for chronological reviews.

3. Add what you saw

Create an account on the device to contribute a gym, a machine, or a review with wait time and notes.

Not official gym data

OnTheRack is a personal project. It has no partnership with any chain, club, or government operator (including LCSD). Listings and ratings are user-generated and may be incomplete, outdated, or wrong.

  • Seeded rows are labelled Sample data
  • Always confirm hours and access with the venue
  • Do not treat reviews as safety or maintenance advice
  • Users are responsible for what they post
